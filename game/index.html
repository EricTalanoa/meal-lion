        How to play

        • Leo is cooking a dish. Guess it from the ingredients.
        • You get one guess per ingredient in the dish. The total stays hidden, but you’ll be told when it’s your last guess.
        • Each wrong guess reveals another ingredient.
        • Skipping reveals an ingredient but uses a guess. You can’t skip your last guess.
        • Pick a dish from the list, then press Guess.
        • Daily dish: one dish for everyone, new at midnight US Eastern. Endless: keep playing other dishes.
